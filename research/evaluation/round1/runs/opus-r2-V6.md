Here's a weekend plan you can adapt. Most of the risk in a weekend move is decided in the weeks before it, so the plan starts there.

## Questions I would ask

1. **Do you own the IT, or is there an IT provider?** Servers, a network closet, phones and printers decide much of the schedule. *Default:* a small setup (laptops, a few desktops, one printer, a router/switch, maybe a NAS) with internet from an ISP. No on-premises servers.
2. **How far is the new building, and what are the access rules?** Loading dock, freight elevator, building hours, a certificate of insurance (COI) for movers. *Default:* same city, under 30 minutes away. Both buildings need booked elevator slots and the movers' COI.
3. **Professional movers or do-it-yourself?** *Default:* a professional moving company for furniture and crates. Staff pack their own desks.
4. **Is the new space ready?** Furniture, cabling, power, internet already installed. *Default:* the fit-out is done, and the ISP circuit is installed and tested **before** the weekend. If it isn't, this becomes the biggest risk in the plan.
5. **Does anything need to keep running over the weekend?** Phones, customer support, deliveries. *Default:* no. Calls forward to mobiles.
6. **Budget, and does staff help over the weekend?** *Default:* a move coordinator plus 2 or 3 volunteers (paid or given time off in lieu). Everyone else only packs their own desk on Friday.

## Roles
- **Move lead** (one person who makes decisions and is the movers' contact).
- **IT lead** (internal or the provider).
- **Building contacts**, old and new (facilities managers).
- **Floor captain at each site** over the weekend.

## Before the weekend

**4 to 6 weeks out**
- Book movers (get 2 or 3 quotes; confirm the date, crate rental, insurance and COI).
- Book elevator and loading-dock slots at both buildings for Friday evening and Saturday.
- Confirm the new ISP install date (at least 1 week before the move) and transfer or set up phone numbers.
- Draw a floor plan of the new office with numbered desks. Assign each person a desk number.
- Plan to update the address with suppliers, the bank, insurance, the website and Google Business profile, and for mail forwarding.

**2 weeks out**
- Check the new space: power outlets, network ports at each desk, Wi-Fi, keys and access cards for all 12 people, alarm codes.
- Do a full IT inventory and a full backup. Test that the backup restores.
- Tell staff the plan: what to pack, the labeling system, the deadline (Friday 4pm), and how Monday morning will work.
- Decide what to get rid of (old furniture, e-waste) and book a pickup.

**Week of the move**
- Deliver crates and labels by Wednesday. Each label shows the desk number and the room.
- Wednesday/Thursday: test internet and Wi-Fi on site at the new office with a laptop.
- Thursday: pack shared areas (kitchen, supply cupboard, archives).
- Confirm movers, elevators and access the day before.

## The weekend

**Friday**
- By 4pm: staff finish packing desks, label everything, and take laptops and personal valuables home.
- 5 to 6pm: IT shuts down and labels the network gear, printer and desktops (photograph the cabling first).
- 6pm onward: movers load the IT equipment and fragile items first, then the crates. They follow the floor-plan map.

**Saturday**
- Morning: unload at the new building. A floor captain directs each item to its numbered desk or room.
- Afternoon: IT sets up the network rack, router, switch and Wi-Fi, then the printer and desktops.
- Final check at the old office: empty, cleaned, nothing left behind. Take photos for the deposit. Return keys or cards, or schedule that for Monday.

**Sunday**
- Unpack shared areas. Put furniture in its final place.
- IT test pass: each desk gets internet, the printer prints, phones ring, the NAS/shared drive is reachable, VPN works, door access works.
- Leave buffer time for anything that broke or went missing on Saturday.
- Afternoon: the move lead walks through and sends staff a "Monday morning" message (address, parking, access, where their desk is).

**Monday 8am**
- Staff arrive and unpack their own crates. IT is on the floor for the first 2 hours to fix problems.
- The movers collect empty crates later in the week.

## Fallback options
- **Internet isn't live:** a 4G/5G router or phone hotspots for Monday, and/or a remote-work day.
- **Movers run late on Saturday:** Sunday is the buffer. Don't plan work for Sunday that's needed for Monday.
- **Lost or damaged items:** the inventory plus photos make insurance claims easy.
