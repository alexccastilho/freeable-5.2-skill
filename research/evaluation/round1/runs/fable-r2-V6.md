# Office move plan: 12 people, Friday 6pm to Monday 8am

The weekend itself is the easy part if the preparation is done. The item that most often sinks a move like this is internet at the new building, because a new line can take weeks to order. Confirm that first, and treat it as the go/no-go for the date.

This plan assumes the defaults listed under "Questions I would ask" at the end. The biggest assumptions are that you are hiring movers, the new space is already fitted out, and you have no server room beyond a network cabinet.

## Roles

| Role | Who | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Move lead | One named person | Owns the plan, holds the keys, makes calls on the weekend |
| IT lead | Internal or your IT provider | Network, printers, phones, workstation setup |
| Building contact, old and new | Landlord or facilities manager | Access, lifts, loading bay, parking |
| Everyone else | All staff | Pack their own desk and test their own setup on Monday |

Two or three people on site over the weekend is enough. The rest of the team should not be needed.

## Before the weekend

**4 or more weeks out**

- Order internet at the new building, with an install date at least a week before the move. Ask for the date in writing.
- Book the movers and get a written quote that covers both buildings, stairs or lifts, and insurance.
- Confirm with both landlords: weekend access, lift bookings, loading bay, parking for the truck, and any rules about move hours.
- Check that your insurance covers goods in transit and the new premises from the Friday.
- Draw a floor plan of the new office with a numbered desk for each person, and check that power and network points match it.

**2 to 3 weeks out**

- Tell staff the date, the plan and what they need to do.
- Start the address changes: mail redirection, bank, insurer, tax and company registrations, suppliers, customers, website, email signatures, map listings.
- Arrange phone number transfer or forwarding.
- Decide what is not coming, and book disposal or recycling for old furniture and equipment.
- Order crates, labels, bubble wrap and cable bags. Movers often rent crates.
- Arrange keys, access cards and alarm codes for the new building.

**The week before**

- IT lead visits the new building and confirms the internet is live. Install the router, switch and Wi-Fi and test them. If the line is not live by Wednesday, set up a backup (a 4G/5G router) or postpone.
- Confirm that backups of anything stored on site are complete and have been tested.
- Label everything with the person's name and new desk number: crates, monitors, chairs, PCs. Colour-code by area if the new office has more than one room.
- Photograph the back of any complicated setup before unplugging it.
- Send the movers the floor plan and a contact number for the weekend.

## The weekend

**Friday**

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| By 3pm | Staff pack personal items and desk contents into labelled crates |
| 3pm to 6pm | Staff shut down, unplug and bag their cables, labelled per desk. Laptops go home with their owners |
| 6pm | Office closes. IT lead disconnects the printers, network gear and any shared equipment |
| 6pm to 9pm | Movers do a first load if they offer an evening slot, otherwise final checks and lock up |

**Saturday: move everything**

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 8am | Movers arrive. Move lead at the old building, a second person at the new one directing items to numbered desks |
| Morning | Furniture and crates loaded and moved. IT equipment travels last and is unloaded first, or goes separately by car |
| Afternoon | Furniture placed according to the floor plan. Crates delivered to desks |
| End of day | Walk through the old office to check that nothing is left behind. Everything should be in the new building by Saturday night |

**Sunday: make it work**

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| Morning | IT lead sets up each desk: monitors, docks, PCs, phones. Connect and test the printers |
| Midday | Test from every desk: power, network, Wi-Fi, printing, phones, door access |
| Afternoon | Meeting room equipment, kitchen, signage, clear the packaging. Fix anything the tests found |
| 5pm | Move lead sends everyone a message: the address, how to get in, where to park, where their desk is, what time to arrive |

Sunday evening and the early hours of Monday are your buffer. The plan should not rely on them.

**Monday**

- 7am: move lead and IT lead on site for a final check.
- 8am: staff arrive, unpack their crates and report problems to one place (a shared list or a single person).
- Keep the first morning free of important client meetings.
- IT lead stays on the floor all day.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fallback |
|---|---|
| Internet is not working | 4G/5G router, or the team works from home on Monday |
| Movers are late or cancel | Sunday absorbs a one-day slip. Have a second mover's number to hand |
| Lift out of service | Ask the movers in advance whether they can handle stairs, and what it costs |
| Something is damaged or missing | Photograph it, note it on the mover's paperwork before signing, claim on insurance |
| Setup is not finished by Sunday night | Prioritise network and desks. Printers and meeting rooms can wait until Monday |

If the team has laptops, working from home on Monday is a real fallback. Tell the team on Friday that this could happen.

## The week after

- Collect and return the crates.
- Hand back the old office: cleaning, repairs, meter readings, keys, and a dated set of photos of its condition.
- Chase any address changes that have not come through, and check that mail redirection is working.
- Cancel services at the old address: internet, cleaning, utilities.

## Questions I would ask

1. **Is the internet at the new building already live?** Default: not yet, so it is the first thing to order and the go/no-go for the date.
2. **How far apart are the buildings?** Default: same city, under an hour's drive, so the movers can make several trips.
3. **Is the furniture moving, or is the new office furnished?** Default: desks, chairs and storage all move.
4. **Do you have servers or other on-site equipment beyond a router, switch and printers?** Default: no, everything is cloud-based. If you do have servers, they need their own shutdown, transport and restart plan, and Sunday gets tighter.
5. **Laptops or desktop PCs?** Default: mostly laptops with a monitor and dock at each desk.
6. **Is the new space ready (power, network cabling, fit-out complete)?** Default: yes. If the cabling is not done, it must be finished before the weekend.
7. **Who handles IT today?** Default: one person in the team or an outside provider who can be booked for the Sunday and Monday.
8. **What is the budget, and are professional movers acceptable?** Default: yes. For 12 desks this is one truck and a crew of three or four for a day.
9. **Does either building restrict weekend access or lift use?** Default: both allow it with advance booking.
10. **When does the old lease end?** Default: there are at least a few days of overlap, so cleaning and handover can happen the week after.
11. **Is anyone working that weekend, or is there anything that cannot be offline (phones, a customer support line)?** Default: no, a full outage from Friday 6pm to Monday 8am is acceptable.
