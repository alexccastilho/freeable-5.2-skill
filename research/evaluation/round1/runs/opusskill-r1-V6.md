# Office move plan: Friday 6pm to Monday 8am

For 12 people, carrying the furniture and boxes is the easy part. A moving crew does it in one day. What decides whether Monday at 8am works is everything that has to be ready in the new building before the truck arrives: the internet connection, building access and lift bookings, and power and data in the right places. The internet line is the one item that cannot move. Providers often need several weeks to install a new circuit, so it gets ordered first, and the weekend itself is planned with a fallback: if the network is not working by Sunday afternoon, Monday becomes a work-from-home day rather than a broken office day.

**Not seen:** I don't know the date, the two buildings, your budget or what equipment you have. The plan rests on the defaults below and works for any weekend you pick.

## Questions I would ask

1. **Which weekend, and is it fixed (for example by the old lease ending)?** Default: not fixed, and at least 6 weeks away. If it is under 4 weeks away, call the internet provider today. If the install cannot be confirmed in time, plan a temporary connection (a 4G/5G router) for the first weeks.
2. **Professional movers, or staff and a rented van?** Default: a professional moving company for furniture and boxes; staff pack their own desks. If you choose to do it yourselves, Saturday needs about 6 to 8 helpers and the plan gains a lift-and-carry safety briefing, and the cost of a damaged item lands on you.
3. **Is there equipment in the office that the business depends on: a server, a NAS, a phone system, a printer under lease?** Default: a router, a small switch, Wi-Fi access points, one leased printer, and everything else in the cloud. If there is an on-site server, it gets its own step: a verified backup Friday, moved by the IT person in a car, not the truck, and started and tested on Saturday.
4. **Budget?** Not set. Rough split for a small office move: movers 40 to 50%, IT and cabling 25 to 35%, new furniture or fit-out 0 to 20%, packing materials and contingency the rest (keep at least 10% contingency). You supply the total.
5. **Who runs it?** Default: one move lead from the team with authority to spend within budget, plus one IT person (staff or a contractor) on site Saturday and Sunday.

Answer first: 1 and 3. The date decides whether the internet line is at risk. An on-site server is the main way the weekend can go wrong.

## Plan

### 6 or more weeks before

- **Order the internet line at the new address** and get a written install date at least one week before the move. Ask the provider to keep the old line running until the Monday after the move. Check: the install date is in writing.
- **Book the movers** for Saturday, with a site visit to both buildings so the quote covers stairs, lift sizes and parking. Ask about their insurance and cancellation terms. Check: signed booking.
- **Building rules, both buildings:** weekend access, lift reservation, loading-bay times, whether a building contact must be present, and the insurance certificate many buildings require from movers. Check: written confirmation of Friday evening and Saturday access at both ends.
- **Floor plan:** who sits where, and where power and network points are needed. Order any cabling or electrical work in the new space now. Check: every desk on the plan has power and either a data point or Wi-Fi coverage.

### 2 to 4 weeks before

- **Tell people what happens and when,** once the movers and the building access are confirmed, not before: the date, what they pack themselves, what the movers handle, where they sit, how to get in on Monday (keys, cards, alarm codes, parking).
- **Change of address:** post redirection, bank, insurance, suppliers, website, Google Business listing, e-mail signatures. Schedule the changes for the Monday of the move.
- **Order packing materials** and label colours or numbers per desk/room matching the floor plan.
- **IT:** confirm the new line is installed and working, the Wi-Fi equipment is ready, and the phone numbers (if any) move with you. Check: someone has loaded a web page over the new line in the new building.
- **Clear out:** decide what is not moving (old furniture, paper archives) and book its disposal.

### Week of the move

- **Thursday: go/no-go.** All of these must be true, or move the date (lose a deposit rather than a working week):
  - New internet line tested on site.
  - Movers, lift booking and building access confirmed for both buildings in writing.
  - Keys or access cards for the new building in hand for at least two people.
  - Backup of anything stored on office equipment completed and checked.
- Staff finish packing by Friday 4pm; each box labelled with destination.

### The weekend

- **Friday 6pm to 9pm:** IT shuts down and packs network gear, printer and any server last, after a final backup. Move lead walks the old office and checks every box is labelled. IT takes the router and any server in a car.
- **Saturday:** movers load in the morning and unload at the new building by afternoon. Move lead at the new building directs boxes to rooms by label. Check by end of day: everything on the inventory has arrived, and damage is noted with the movers before they leave.
- **Sunday morning:** IT sets up the network, Wi-Fi and printer, and tests each desk position. Desks and chairs assembled; boxes placed at each desk (staff unpack their own).
- **Sunday 4pm: decision point.** If the internet, Wi-Fi and building access are all working, Monday is in the office. If any of them is not, the move lead messages everyone that Monday is a work-from-home day, and IT keeps working on it. Deciding at 4pm on Sunday gives people the evening to plan instead of finding out at the door.
- **Sunday evening:** old office emptied and swept, keys ready to hand back, meter readings photographed.

### Monday

- 7:30am: move lead and IT in the building before everyone else.
- First hour: a short check of each person's setup; collect problems in one list.
- Keep the old internet line until the end of the week in case of rollback, then cancel. Hand back the old keys per the lease.

## Found, not touched

- Leases often require the old office to be returned in a stated condition (repainting, removing cabling). Read the exit clause now; that work may need its own date before the lease ends.
- Insurance: check that contents are covered during the move and at the new address from the Saturday.

## Need from you

- The move weekend date and whether it is fixed.
- A list of the office equipment, especially anything on-site that people depend on.
- The building contacts (managers or landlords) for both addresses.
- The budget total and whether you want movers.
