# Two-day offsite for 40 people: plan

Three months is workable but tight for 40 people. Venues that can host a group this size book out 2 to 4 months ahead, so choosing the venue is the first thing on the critical path. Below is a full plan built on stated defaults. Change any default and the rest adjusts.

## Questions I would ask

1. **What is the offsite for?** Strategy and planning, team bonding, a mix, or a celebration? Default: a mix, about 50% structured work sessions and 50% team-building and social time.
2. **Budget per person?** Default: about USD 600 to 900 per person all-in (venue, one night's lodging, meals, activities, local transport), excluding flights. Roughly USD 24k to 36k total.
3. **Where are people based?** All in one city, or distributed? Default: most in one city, a few travelling in. The venue is within 1.5 to 2 hours by ground transport.
4. **Overnight?** Default: yes, one night (day 1 arrival to day 2 departure), shared lodging at the venue.
5. **Dates.** Any fixed constraints such as product launches, holidays or fiscal-year end? Default: a Wednesday to Thursday about 12 weeks from now, avoiding public holidays.
6. **Who owns this?** Is there an organizer or team, and who approves spend? Default: you are the organizer with one helper, and a single exec sponsor approves the budget and agenda.
7. **Constraints.** Accessibility needs, dietary restrictions, partners invited or not, alcohol policy? Default: employees only, accessible venue required, collect dietary needs at registration, moderate alcohol at the dinner only.

## Phase 1: Define (week 1)

- One-page brief: 2 or 3 goals (for example "align on next year's priorities", "cross-team relationships"), what success looks like, budget, dates, audience.
- Get the brief signed off by the sponsor. Every later decision is checked against it.
- Send a **save-the-date** with the two dates and "details to follow". Ask people to flag conflicts within a week.

## Phase 2: Venue (weeks 2–3, critical path)

Requirements for 40 people:
- A main room for 40 in a flexible layout (cabaret or round tables), plus 3 or 4 breakout rooms for 8 to 12 people each.
- AV: projector or screen, microphones, reliable Wi-Fi for 40+ devices.
- About 40 bedrooms (or ~20 twins if sharing is acceptable. Ask; many people prefer single rooms).
- On-site catering, an outdoor or activity space, step-free access.
- Contract: watch the **attrition clause** (how far below the guaranteed headcount you can go), the cancellation schedule, the food-and-beverage minimum, and the final-numbers deadline.

Process: shortlist 5 venues, send a standard RFP with dates, headcount, room needs and budget. Compare 3 quotes in one table, visit 1 or 2, then sign. Hold a second-choice date if you can.

## Phase 3: Content and activities (weeks 4–8)

**Draft agenda**

*Day 1*
- 09:00–10:00: travel and arrival, coffee
- 10:00–10:30: opening: why we're here, goals, agenda (exec sponsor)
- 10:30–12:30: session 1, the big topic (for example strategy review), plenary plus discussion
- 12:30–13:30: lunch
- 13:30–15:30: session 2, breakout workshops (mixed teams, 4 groups of 10)
- 15:30–16:00: break
- 16:00–17:30: team activity (outdoor or a facilitated activity: cooking class, orienteering, workshop)
- 17:30–19:00: free time / check-in
- 19:00–22:00: group dinner, short recognition moment

*Day 2*
- 08:00–09:00: breakfast
- 09:00–10:30: session 3, breakout read-outs and prioritization
- 10:30–11:00: break
- 11:00–12:30: session 4, commitments: owners, next steps, open Q&A with leadership
- 12:30–13:30: lunch
- 13:30–14:30: closing and retrospective of the offsite
- 14:30: depart

Principles: no session longer than 2 hours without a break, at least one unstructured block each day, and the activity is something people of all fitness levels and interests can take part in. Assign each session an owner who prepares it and sends any pre-reads by week 11.

Consider an external facilitator for the strategy sessions (book by week 5) so leaders can take part instead of running the room.

## Phase 4: Logistics (weeks 4–10)

- **Registration form** (week 4): attendance, dietary needs and allergies, accessibility needs, rooming preference, T-shirt size if you are doing swag, emergency contact, transport (coach versus own travel).
- **Transport:** a chartered coach (about 50 seats) from the office if the venue is out of town. For people travelling in, book flights by week 6 for better fares.
- **Catering:** confirm the menu against the dietary data. Provide clear labels and enough non-alcoholic options.
- **Rooming list** to the venue by its deadline (usually 2 to 3 weeks before).
- **Supplies:** name badges, flipcharts, markers, sticky notes, printed agendas, a first-aid kit, power strips.

## Phase 5: Final weeks (11–12)

- Final headcount to all vendors by their deadlines.
- Run-of-show document: minute-by-minute timings, who is responsible for each slot, vendor contacts, room assignments, a wet-weather backup for outdoor activities, and an emergency plan (nearest hospital, who holds the first-aid kit).
- Brief the 2 or 3 helpers. Name a point of contact for attendees.
- Send attendees a "know before you go" email: agenda, address, transport times, what to pack, dress code, Wi-Fi details.

## Phase 6: After (week 13)

- 5-question survey within 48 hours.
- Share the commitments and owners from session 4, with a follow-up date 30 days out.
- Thank the vendors and helpers, and reconcile the budget.

## Key risks

- **Venue availability.** Start the RFPs this week. Late booking is the most common failure.
- **Attrition penalties** if attendance drops. Negotiate the guaranteed number at about 85% of 40.
- **Agenda overload.** Most offsites try to cover too much. Two big topics is plenty.
- **Weather** for outdoor activities. Always have an indoor backup.
